Hi, I'm Xoan M. Otero Jorge, aka XoanOuteiro. A 23yo passionate Ethical Hacker, Full Stack developer and Linux Enthusiast. I created this blog to share my notes, tutorials, small tools & programs.

I'm currently working at Secur0, a Bug Bounty Startup where I work as somewhat of an offensive security lead doing both webapp pentests & triage for Vulnerability Disclosure Programs and Bug Bounty Programs.

We are also a CNA, so I have managed the disclosure of quite a bunch of CVEs. We do so mostly for external researchers so I'm commonly tagged as analyst.

On the lamer side of things I'm also a CS degree student at Vigo University, Ourense Campus. Though I've started this degree mostly for fun as I already have vocational training as a full stack dev (FP DAM) and an IT Cybersecurity Specialist (FP CETI).

I'm very fond of aiding people, communities etc in whatever they may need, I love community building and helping in any way I can, if you have a project, or want to chat feel welcome to do so. Do keep in mind I'd rather work in open communities where people are not discriminated in any manner.
